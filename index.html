01 — MISSIONWHY WE EXIST

We turn abstract fintech ideas into rooms full of people arguing about IPL bids and Bitcoin — because that's how the concepts actually stick.

Three in leadership, eight running the portfolios between them. Two faculty conveners keeping us (mostly) legal.

Learn by doing

No lectures about liquidity. You price a player nobody else wants, you defend a pitch in public, you ask the crypto guy the rude question.

/ 02

Built for outsiders

WE REPLY FROM CAMPUS. USUALLY WITHIN A WEEK, ALWAYS WITH OPINIONS.

No. Curiosity and a willingness to argue about IPL auctions is preferred. The committee is built for students who'd rather learn finance by breaking it a little.

One of three formats: a mock auction with a fintech valuation twist, a talk from someone who works in the space, or a pitch night where teams get taken apart in public. Whichever it is, you will be asked an opinion.

Eleven students across leadership and six portfolios, under the guidance of our faculty conveners — Dr. Nayana Mahajan and Prof. Manoj Suryavanshi.
